King EX80 1.0.0 by The Disk King

**King EX80 by The Disk King: Inspired by the Akai EX80E enhancer (1987). Generates new upper harmonics to brighten dull sounds: INPUT with an input light, INTENSITY (how much the harmonic generator is driven), FILTER 1 to 5 kHz (12 dB/oct, the band that feeds it), MIX (how much is added), EFFECT in/out.**

![King EX80 1.0.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/main/king-ex80/king-ex80.png)

Part of **The King Rack**: six free 80s half-rack effects inspired by Akai's 1987-88 EX series. Get all six:
https://github.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/tree/main/king-rack

**It is an EFFECT: find it in Insert Effects** (Mixer or a track's Insert Effects → External Plugins → The Disk King → King EX80). Tested on Gen 1 (Akai MPC X SE) and Gen 2 (Akai MPC Live III), MPC OS 3.10.1.

Downloads: **Gen 1** (32-bit: Live, Live II, One, One+, X, Key 61, Key 37, Force) `*-mpc-armv7.zip`, **Gen 2** (64-bit: Live III, XL) `*-mpc-aarch64.zip`, or `*-mpc-sdk.zip` that works on both.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - King EX80` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with **MPC Plugins Manager** (https://mpcplugins.github.io/p/mpc-plugins-manager/): it also brings you every update.
