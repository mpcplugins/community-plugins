King Phatt 0.2.0

New KEYS page, HELP popup and load-your-own-bank support.

- **KEYS page (7th tab):** on-screen keyboard with Key 61 and Key 37 ranges. Pads light the matching key; tap a key on screen to play it.
- **HELP popup** on MAIN: where the sounds come from and how to load your own bank.
- **Load your own E-mu .exb bank:** make a folder `Hybrid Phatt Sets` at the top of any MPC drive and copy your bank in: `Hybrid Phatt Sets/My Bank/My Bank.exb` plus its `SamplePool` folder.
- LCD shows LAYERS L1-L4 (lit when used, flashing while playing).
- Values show units; PREV / NEXT step one preset per tap and no longer stick.

No sound bank is included (copyright). MOD, GROOVE and FX are on screen but do not change the sound yet.

Tested on Gen 1 (Akai MPC X SE) and Gen 2 (Akai MPC Live III), MPC OS 3.10.1.

Downloads: **Gen 1** (32-bit: Live, Live II, One, One+, X, Key 61, Key 37, Force) `*-mpc-armv7.zip`, **Gen 2** (64-bit: Live III, XL) `*-mpc-aarch64.zip`, or `*-mpc-sdk.zip` that works on both.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - Hybrid Phatt` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager.
