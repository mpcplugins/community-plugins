Hybrid Phatt 0.1.0 by The Disk King

A 90s-style ROM sound module for the MPC: 4 banks of 128 presets, layered multi-sample voices with key and velocity splits, 6-stage envelopes, and a hardware-style screen that crosses an old MPC 60 with a 90s sound module.

**Early beta.** Tested on an Akai MPC Live III, MPC OS 3.10.1.

- Six pages: MAIN, LAYERS, FILTER, MOD, GROOVE, FX, with Q-Links in groups of four.
- No sound bank is included in 0.1: the plugin plays a test tone and the LCD says "SOUND BANK NOT FOUND". A bundled sound set is planned.
- MOD, GROOVE and FX are on screen but do not change the sound yet.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - Hybrid Phatt` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager.
