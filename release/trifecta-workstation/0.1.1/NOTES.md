King Triton Workstation 0.1.1

**New KEYS page**, rebuilt to look like a real keyboard.

- Realistic keys: ivory keys with a shadow under the felt and a bevelled front edge; black keys with shaded sides, a glossy top and a shadow on the white keys. Keys you hit on the pads (or tap) light up blue.
- The big VALUE dial is the **MASTER VOLUME** on the KEYS page.
- The screen is centred, with PREV / NEXT beside it and the bank keys A-D right under it.

Still early beta. Every program plays the built-in Trifecta Voice. The original Korg Triton sounds cannot be included: loading your own sounds is planned for 0.2. Tested on Gen 1 (Akai MPC X SE) and Gen 2 (Akai MPC Live III), MPC OS 3.10.1.

Downloads: **Gen 1** (32-bit: Live, Live II, One, One+, X, Key 61, Key 37, Force) `*-mpc-armv7.zip`, **Gen 2** (64-bit: Live III, XL) `*-mpc-aarch64.zip`, or `*-mpc-sdk.zip` that works on both.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - Trifecta Workstation` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager.
