Warm Front Compressor 1.0.0 by The Disk King

**First release.** A vintage analog-style stereo compressor for the MPC, with 27 presets.

![Warm Front Compressor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/main/warm-front-compressor/warm-front-compressor.png)

- **Three styles:** CLASSIC (1980s console bus glue with AUTO release), VINTAGE (smooth opto/tube-style warmth), MODERN (clean and fast, with look-ahead).
- **THRESHOLD, RATIO, ATTACK, RELEASE (with AUTO), MAKEUP GAIN and KNEE** knobs, LINK, and a SIDECHAIN FILTER so bass does not make it pump.
- **INPUT / OUTPUT VU meters in real dB** (METER GR shows gain reduction on the OUTPUT needle), plus a 12-light GAIN REDUCTION strip.
- **27 presets.** 19 copy the published settings of famous studio compressors: the SSL G-Series bus compressor, the 1176 (including "all buttons in"), the LA-2A, the Fairchild 660/670 time constants, the Manley Variable Mu, the dbx 160, the API 2500 and the Neve 33609. Plus 8 Warm Front originals such as **Mix Glue (Bus)**. The full list, with every setting, the hardware it is based on, and who is known for using it, is in the [README](https://github.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/tree/main/warm-front-compressor#all-27-presets).
- HELP card on the panel, Q-Links mapped to the knobs.

**It is an EFFECT: find it in Insert Effects** (Mixer or a track's Insert Effects → External Plugins → The Disk King → Warm Front Compressor). On a pad it squeezes that sample, on a track the whole track, on the Master your whole beat. Pick a preset from the preset name at the top of the plugin window. Tested on an Akai MPC Live III, MPC OS 3.10.1.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - Warm Front Compressor` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager.
