dkx 160 Compressor 1.0.1 by The Disk King

**Presets move the panel, and a blank Default.**

![dkx 160 Compressor 1.0.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/main/dkx-160-compressor/dkx-160-compressor.png)

- **Presets move the panel:** pick a preset and the knobs, switches and buttons on the panel go to its settings (OUTPUT, THRESHOLD and COMPRESSION knobs, POWER and the meter switch).
- **Starts on Default:** a new "Default" preset (nothing set) is first, and the plugin loads on it until you pick a preset.
- Everything else as in 1.0.0. Your projects still open.

**It is an EFFECT: find it in Insert Effects** (Mixer or a track's Insert Effects → External Plugins → The Disk King → dkx 160 Compressor). Tested on an Akai MPC Live III, MPC OS 3.10.1.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - dkx 160 Compressor` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager (it also brings you every update). Updating: replace the old folder, then remove the plugin from its insert slot and add it again.
