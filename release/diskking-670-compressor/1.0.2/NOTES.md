Diskking 670 Compressor 1.0.2 by The Disk King

**Presets move the panel, a blank Default, and two art fixes.**

![Diskking 670 Compressor 1.0.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mpcplugins/community-plugins/main/diskking-670-compressor/diskking-670-compressor.png)

- **Presets move the panel:** pick a preset and the knobs, switches and buttons on the panel go to its settings.
- **Starts on Default:** a new "Default" preset (nothing set) is first, and the plugin loads on it until you pick a preset.
- **LEFT-LAT THRESHOLD has its arrow** on the outer ring, like RIGHT-VERT, turning with the knob.
- **A white speck** on the screw under RIGHT-VERT ZERO is gone.
- Everything else as in 1.0.1. Your projects still open.

**It is an EFFECT: find it in Insert Effects** (Mixer or a track's Insert Effects → External Plugins → The Disk King → Diskking 670 Compressor). Tested on Gen 1 (Akai MPC X SE) and Gen 2 (Akai MPC Live III), MPC OS 3.10.1.

Downloads: **Gen 1** (32-bit: Live, Live II, One, One+, X, Key 61, Key 37, Force) `*-mpc-armv7.zip`, **Gen 2** (64-bit: Live III, XL) `*-mpc-aarch64.zip`, or `*-mpc-sdk.zip` that works on both.

Install: copy `The Disk King - VST - Diskking 670 Compressor` into `VST2 Plugins` on your MPC's internal drive (MPC OS 3.10.1+, Enhancements and External Plugin Support on), or install it with MPC Plugins Manager (it also brings you every update). Updating: replace the old folder, then remove the plugin from its insert slot and add it again.
